        Tap a note to select it; shift-click or drag a box to select more. Drag notes to move them, and a note's edge or the handles beside a single selected note to resize it. Keys in the roll: Delete, Up/Down a step in key, Shift+Up/Down an octave, Alt+Up/Down a semitone, Left/Right a sixteenth (Shift: a beat), Alt+Left/Right shorter/longer, N a new note, Ctrl+A all, Esc none. + Note adds one after the last note.

        PhrasesPhrase tools

        A phrase ends at a rest of a beat or more at the current tempo. Choose one to move, transpose or copy it; the harmony is not changed, so generate it again afterwards.
        MIDI keyboard
        Keys you play sound on the melody instrument. Record counts in one bar with a click at the tempo, plays the song, and adds what you play to the melody as one edit you can undo. Played to a MIDI device, the melody goes on the chosen channel, the harmony on the next one, the harmony voice on the one after and the drums on channel 10.

        Change a chordChord symbols

        The symbols above the chords roll are read from its notes, a chord a bar or more where one changes inside a bar. Choose one, then a root and kind or a chord of the key: its notes move to the new chord, keeping their rhythm, as one edit you can undo.

        Generated from the melody, one chord per 4/4 bar on the song's bars, the starter beat's; generate again to replace the part. Tap a note to select it; shift-click or drag a box to select more. Keys in the roll: Delete, Up/Down a step in key, Shift+Up/Down an octave, Alt+Up/Down a semitone, Left/Right a sixteenth (Shift: a beat), Alt+Left/Right shorter/longer, N a new note, Ctrl+A all, Esc none.

        Edit the notesHarmony voice roll

        A second line that plays with the melody: duplicate the melody, or the notes selected in it, a third or a sixth away in the key or an octave away, then shift or edit the copy here. Create harmony offers lines made for the melody instead: thirds above first (a sixth where the third is not in the chord), then a sixth below, the tune an octave lower, a counter-melody and two voices with Try another; each plays at once and is shown here, and Keep puts it in place of these notes. It is a part of its own, so the chords or bass line stay as they are.         Describe how you want the music to change: Tandem sends the song's notes and settings to an AI model, never your recordings.

        What is sent

        Your whole project (notes, rhythms, lyrics, song sections, genre, mixer settings, the band's description and the names of your takes and layers, but no recordings) and the recent chat go to the configured AI model, and while a practice pass is marked on the melody, its scores. You review the edits by hearing them.

        Your AI

        Use your own AI account. The key is kept in this Mac's Keychain and sent only to the provider you choose, and only over https:// unless the server is on this Mac; Tandem never shows it again or writes it into projects.

        Connection (advanced)

        The AI service runs on the same Vercel deployment. Leave the API server field empty unless connecting to another compatible backend. AI credentials stay on the server.

        The token is remembered only for this tab and forgotten when the tab closes.

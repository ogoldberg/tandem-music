    Melody
    Tap a note to select it; shift-click or drag a box to select more. Drag notes to move them, and a note's edge or the handles beside a single selected note to resize it. Keys in the roll: Delete, Up/Down a step in key, Shift+Up/Down an octave, Alt+Up/Down a semitone, Left/Right a sixteenth (Shift: a beat), Alt+Left/Right shorter/longer, N a new note, Ctrl+A all, Esc none. + Note adds one after the last note.
    Phrases
    A phrase ends at a rest of a beat or more at the current tempo. Choose one to move, transpose or copy it; the harmony is not changed, so generate it again afterwards.
    Drums
    Tap a drum hit to remove it.
    Harmony
    Generated from the melody, one chord per 4/4 bar starting at the first note; generate again to replace the part. Tap a note to select it; shift-click or drag a box to select more. Drag notes to move them, and a note's edge or the handles beside a single selected note to resize it. Keys in the roll: Delete, Up/Down a step in key, Shift+Up/Down an octave, Alt+Up/Down a semitone, Left/Right a sixteenth (Shift: a beat), Alt+Left/Right shorter/longer, N a new note, Ctrl+A all, Esc none.
    Harmony voice
    A second line that plays with the melody: duplicate the melody, or the notes selected in it, a third or a sixth away in the key or an octave away, then shift or edit the copy here. Create harmony offers lines made for the melody instead: thirds above first (a sixth where the third is not in the chord), then a sixth below, the tune an octave lower, a counter-melody and two voices with Try another; each plays at once and is shown here, and Keep puts it in place of these notes. It is a part of its own, so the chords or bass line stay as they are. Keys in the roll: Delete, Up/Down a step in key, Shift+Up/Down an octave, Alt+Up/Down a semitone, Left/Right a sixteenth (Shift: a beat), Alt+Left/Right shorter/longer, N a new note, Ctrl+A all, Esc none.
    MIDI keyboard
    Keys you play sound on the melody instrument. Record counts in one bar with a click at the tempo, plays the song, and adds what you play to the melody as one edit you can undo. Played to a MIDI device, the melody goes on the chosen channel, the harmony on the next one, the harmony voice on the one after and the drums on channel 10.
